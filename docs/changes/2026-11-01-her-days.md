# Her days and notebook archive

## Changes

Add local activity records with bounded text, dated reads, ratings and forgetting by related user. Add notebook section reads and archive entries deleted during notebook writing. The archive retains the original entry and deletion time; forgetting a person also removes matching archived entries using the notebook's origin rule.

The card's `_forget_person` is the current `_forget` in `mixin_discretion.py`; the new deletion calls sit beside likeness deletion. Failures are logged without stopping the remaining deletion. No plugin methods, switches, prompts or page files were added or changed. Archived entries and activity records are not included in prompts.
