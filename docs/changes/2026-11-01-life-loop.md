## Changes

- `life_loop.py` adds the minute observer, five event sources, one life model
  call per attempt, cooldown, daily cap, budget gates and conditional menus.
  State lives in `states/life.json`. The due table is empty until 1d uses it.
- Choices update her current activity and her day records. Names are anonymized
  before storage. Related people remain deletable through the existing store.
  Meals and rest update bodily needs. Photos run in tracked background tasks.
  Posts use the latest photo from her own life choices today, if one exists.
- `mixin_selfie.py` keeps the reply tool's twelve-character limit. Life choices
  add `kind` and cleaned descriptions of at most forty characters. Sleep choices
  last for their selected duration. Other existing validity rules remain.
- `body.py` uses selected activities instead of scheduled sleep and meals while
  they remain valid. Sleep recovery is continuous. Waking marks the end without
  recovering the same hours again. Bodily checkpoints cover sleep across restart.
- `mixin_core.py` binds the body's optional life state. `mixin_autonomy.py` starts
  life for Her without requiring a model during initialization. Each tick checks
  the current switch and model. A module observer wraps the existing message
  handler to read structured mentions; silence detection reads window timestamps.
  No plugin class method was added.
- `mixin_browse.py` and `mixin_group_browse.py` consume one-shot choices at their
  background entries. Their old scheduling remains when life is off. The trip
  implementation itself was not changed.
- `mixin_llm.py` writes the notebook at the first evening sleep choice, with a
  03:00 fallback and one sitting record per evening. The old notebook time remains
  when life is off. `app.py` also cancels life, life photos, sticker vector and
  relook tasks, and the idle image task.
- `_conf_schema.json` adds `life_loop_enabled`, default false. `common.py`
  registers the `life` function. Its function switch defaults true; the separate
  master switch keeps life inactive by default.

Only authorized Python/configuration files, the new test, this note and the two

dependencies or real data changed.
