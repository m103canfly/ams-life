# 2026-10-29 · emis-look

Her look: the panel and the star map in 月白 on clear glass, and no BioCore wording on screen. The owner chose
"v2丙月白" after six palettes on the fifth (clearest) glass, and asked for no glow around words ("字体不要加光晕，
这样更难看清了"). Only computer screens (the owner: "不需要适配手机端").

- `pages/dashboard/look.css` (new), loaded after `styles.css`: type 12/13/14/16/20/28, corners 6/10/16, shadows
  flat/lifted/popped, spacing in 4s; layers by glass thickness (side bar and groups thin, cards thick, the big card
  and the chosen tab popped); 月白 colours, light and dark; no `text-shadow` anywhere. Without `backdrop-filter`
  (`@supports`) or with `prefers-reduced-transparency` every glass is an opaque card; with
  `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing lifts, presses or slides.
- `pages/dashboard/look.js` (new): her pictures, asked once from `page/look` after the bridge is ready (`app.js`,
  one line), only `data:image/png|jpeg|webp` under 6 MB; the glass capsule that slides under the side tabs.
  Without pictures: a drawn ring and star in the corner, the big card in its plain layout.
- `ui_look.py` (new) and `page/look` (`mixin_pages.py`, GET, read only): the avatar and the half-body art from
  `<data dir>/ui/` (`avatar.png`, `art.png`, or `.jpg` / `.webp`), as data URLs; anything missing, over 4 MB, a
  link, or not starting like a picture is left out. The pictures are Kuro Games' art: never in this repository,
  copied in by the owner when installing.
- Contrast: every word on screen measured against the pixels behind it (light and dark, wide and 900 px, with
  and without her pictures, and the opaque way out). To reach 4.5:1 (3:1 for large words): the second-faintest
  and faintest greys one step deeper, her art behind the glass fainter (light .22, dark .20), a deeper cyan for
  small numbers in light (`--accent2-text`), a deeper pink under white words on the main button (`--accent-strong`).
- Star map (`tools/brain/src/page/brain.css`, copied to `pages/starmap/` and `pages/dashboard/brain/` by the
  page step of `build.mjs`; the bundle is not rebuilt): the same colours, glass on the bar and the side column
  only (nothing new blurs the map), rounder boxes, the title bar kept on one line inside the panel. The names
  floating among the stars keep the thin outline they always had.
- `pages/dashboard/orb.js`: the signal line takes the page's accent colour instead of a fixed orange.
- `pages/dashboard/brain_frame.js` (a fix): after answering the map's hello the panel also sends the theme. The
  map only noted the panel's light/dark from the hello, so a light panel showed the map's bar and side dark
  around a light map until the theme was switched once.
- `logo.png`: BioCore's icon replaced by a drawn pink star in a cyan ring (no character art).
- Wording (earlier commits on this branch): 小爱的内心世界, 她的状态, ams-life's own version, the "about" line
  with the licence and the source; "update" no longer points at BioCore's repository (no Gitee by default; the
  note says ams-life is installed by the development window).

Behaviour seen by the plugin: one new read-only route, `page/look`. The golden traces were re-recorded for that
alone (the list of registered routes gained one line).
