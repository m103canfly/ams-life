# Sticker look v2

The vision prompt asks what a sticker means in conversation and what it replies to.

version marker when present. Offers show the meaning and up to two tags; old rows
still show their caption and scene. Personal images and group jokes are excluded
from candidates. The keep decision and settings are unchanged.

stickers wire (29), segments (4), neutral send (6), golden (2), and basics (11).
