# 2026-10-30 cx-photo: daily life and photo context

## Why

A photo trial showed that the picture and the reply were sent separately and did not fit each other. The owner decided in item 69 that she lives like a person, at home, outside and at meals; item 70 calls for the picture and its caption to be sent together after the text.

## What changed

- This round implements the first three parts: one shared persistent present activity, ordinary-life prompts, and photo context, parameters and queued caption. `set_now` lets her report a change herself; neither another person's words nor keyword guesses update it.
- The fourth part now sends the text normally, waits for the remaining text, and sends a successful photo and its caption in one final message. A failed or timed-out photo sends no caption. Voice remains separate.
- The webpage-sharing tool sends the title, link and caption together as one message after all reply text, subject to the same 60-second wait. Its tool result describes the link accurately; it does not claim that a screenshot image is sent.
- With intent injection off, the present activity is in the transient system prompt so it never enters message history. With injection on it is in the transient `_no_save` intent block.
- The unused image trigger words setting remains for old configurations and its description says so.
- The daily schedule uses local time derived from `time.time()` and a private date-and-period random seed. A schedule selection is saved for its period. Her own update lasts at least 60 minutes, then expires at the first period boundary at or after that minimum, or after three hours, whichever comes first.

| Time | Possible activity |
| --- | --- |
| 00:00–07:00 | Sleep or prepare to sleep at home |
| 07:00–08:00 | Just got up at home |
| 08:00–09:00 | Breakfast at home |
| 09:00–12:00 | Academy class, reading at home, errands or shopping |
| 12:00–14:00 | Lunch at home or outside |
| 14:00–18:00 | Academy class, resting at home, errands or shopping with friends |
| 18:00–20:00 | Dinner at home or outside |
| 20:00–24:00 | Rest, reading or preparing to sleep at home |

Low energy (`<= 0.25`), low mood (`v <= -0.35`) or high stress (`>= 0.60`) add six home-rest choices to the daytime periods; these thresholds follow the compact body interpretation. `HER_MIN_MINUTES` is 60 and `HER_MAX_HOURS` is 3. These are heuristics, not a claim about an actual person's schedule. `PHOTO_WAIT_SECONDS` is 150 to bound slow image generation; `REST_WAIT_SECONDS` is 60 so stuck earlier text does not leave media waiting indefinitely. A photo service call already running in a worker thread may continue after the wait times out, but its result is not sent.

## Switches and scope

No new switch. The present activity and `set_now` follow the plugin switch; photo and screenshot tools retain their existing switches. The unused setting remains compatible. The README's obsolete body description was corrected; no other work outside the card and the requested small fix was intended. The golden recordings were re-recorded with `AMS_UPDATE_GOLDEN=1`. They changed in three places, all from this card: every reply prompt gains the `此刻：…` line before `PIECES_NOTE`; `states/now.json` appears (the schedule's present activity); and the log gains one `phone tools unavailable` warning a turn, because `set_now` is now offered every turn and the test harness has no AstrBot `FunctionTool` (AstrBot itself has it, as for the existing phone tools).
