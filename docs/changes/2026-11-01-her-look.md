# Her look

Her appearance keeps its underlying features while she chooses her outfit and
records lasting changes. With `photo_enabled`, the phone offers `set_look`;
selfies require an outfit for the local day, and photo prompts use her outfit
and recent changes. The turn includes the outfit, season and recent changes.
The weather hook returns no text until a later implementation supplies it.
With the switch off, the new tool and turn line are absent.

`look.py` holds the date, persistence and formatting helpers. The
`look` default in `mixin_people.py` is needed because the self-model loader
only restores keys present in its default dictionary; without it, changes saved
to the self model would be lost on reload. The tool handler is a local
function, so the plugin method count is unchanged.
