# 2026-10-30 — cx-slang

Group slang learning in the plugin. `slang_enabled` defaults off and `slang_image_api_model` defaults empty. The image API adapter reads its endpoint and key from the existing image configuration for each request. Slang model calls have a separate `slang_calls` counter and spending category; they do not increase the vision count.

- Group files under `slang/` hold learned meanings, owner deletions and a 14-day hit ledger. Each hit contains only a phrase, local person identifier, message identifier and time, never a sentence. Examples for the model come only from the current phone window.
- Candidates require five messages from three people. Common words, known names including hers and the owner's, numbers, URLs, placeholders, red lines, private material and forgotten people are excluded. Source narrowing and owner deletion withdraw hits and recalculate the threshold. Unused learned phrases fade after 60 days.
- Other groups are the reference. A phrase must occur at least five times as frequently in the group, with at least two other groups, 20 reference messages in total, and a one-message smoothing prior. Frequencies use distinct message IDs present in the bounded ledgers; they are estimates, not all-message corpus frequencies. With an insufficient reference, no new phrases are learned. A shorter phrase is merged when at least 80% of its message IDs overlap a longer eligible phrase. At most two new phrases are learned per group per night (up to 28 in fourteen nightly runs). Boundary particles and pronouns, and standalone time and quantity expressions, are filtered before ledger insertion. The ledger keeps at most 20,000 hits per group and 200 per phrase, retaining the newest; Chinese candidate fragments are 2–6 characters.
- The reply block is limited to matching words from that group. Unknown words can be asked about when addressed, with a group limit of two prompts per day. The panel exposes a list and delete endpoint with numbered group labels; deleting blocks relearning.
- `tools/sim_slang.py --chat <path>` replays a SQLite `messages` table using the same phrase, cap and ranking functions. It needs a `user_id` or `sender_id` column in addition to the specified message columns to enforce the three-person threshold; without one it reports an error instead of inventing senders. `--exclude-name` may be repeated, and `--self-id` excludes her messages. `--show` opts into displaying up to ten phrases locally. The old chat table does not itself supply the plugin's privacy annotations or name index, so replay cannot reproduce those filters unless supplied separately.
- This borrows the idea of counting repeated group expressions and guessing meanings from context from self-learning. No self-learning code was copied.
