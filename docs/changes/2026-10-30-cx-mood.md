## What

- `body.py` keeps a table of bounded event changes and discounts repeated
  events. It records only event kind and time for the panel; `fun_page` is the
  entry point for a future browsing integration. Stress and energy event effects
  are temporary offsets on the existing derived values.
- `on_message` recognises conservative cues and marks whether a reply is
  directed. `on_message_decide` runs later: when attention determines that an
  uncalled message will not request the model, `mixin_attention.py` observes it
  on a copy of the body and applies bounded emotional changes. Familiar speakers
  have full weight, other conversations one third. When self-decide is off,
  uncalled messages are processed in `on_message`.
- A called message or a message selected for a model request is left to the
  existing `on_llm_request` call to `core.observe`, provided intent injection is
  enabled. The existing request and response handlers are unchanged.
- The five-minute phone sweep checks direct replies for a response. The owner
  is closest; other people use attention familiarity. Each reply can cause
  `answered` only once, tracked on the reply line. The direct flag is also on
  the line, not in its origin.
  Broadcast replies are excluded. Night transitions are recorded without names
  or message text and reduce distress once after the end of night.
- `body_brief` describes recent effects without telling the model how to answer.
  The event description precedes the original body details. The dashboard
  shows at most five event kinds and their times. Attention's
  threshold and wait follow mood and energy within fixed limits.

## Media handoff (review follow-up)

`_note_she_sent(event, kind)` records only a fixed kind after a successful send.
It marks the media line checked and marks the associated written reply as a
share, so the reply and its media cannot cause two unanswered events. It must be
called at the actual send-success points; the former `_media_after_reply`
wrapper and temporary replacement of `event.send` have been removed. The merged
phone implementation calls it after successful photo, screenshot, and voice
sends, using `selfie`, `snap`, `screenshot`, or `voice` as appropriate.
