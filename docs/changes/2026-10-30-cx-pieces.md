# 2026-10-30 cx-pieces: send replies in pieces

The owner asked: “真人不会换行继续打字，而是分段发”. Ordinary reply lines and
the existing 【下一条】 separator become consecutive messages. Consecutive list
lines and fenced code retain their internal line breaks. Extra pieces join the
fifth with spaces. A selected sticker follows its own piece. Queued photos,
voice and screenshots start after the remaining text messages and the existing
media delay. A later text send failure stops the remaining text. The window and
history retain one reply; group counts track the messages sent.

History is settled for an actually split reply; an ordinary single reply keeps
the previous behavior.

`reply_in_pieces` defaults to true. `MAX_MESSAGES` remains 5 and `PIECES_NOTE`
is the per-turn instruction. The self-decision switch still controls silence and
the other self-decision choices. AstrBot's segmented reply setting is untouched.
The golden recordings were regenerated externally. They changed in two places:
the per-turn prompt gained `PIECES_NOTE`, and `states/attention.json` appeared
because group messages are now counted even with self-decision off.

Offline coverage includes `test_split_at_most_five`,
`test_newlines_lists_and_code_blocks`, `test_faces_stay_in_their_piece`,
`test_pieces_without_self_decide_and_switch_off`, and
`test_pieces_stop_after_send_failure`. The externally run suite passed all 777
tests. Two external mutation checks made tests fail as intended: restricting
splitting to self-decision and joining excess pieces with newlines.

Follow-up tests: `test_split_reply_is_one_complete_astrbot_history_entry`,
